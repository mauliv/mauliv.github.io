## Overview

Official press report published by the Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta Public Relations Directorate (*Humas UNY*) highlighting the innovative **Vircon Classroom (Virtual-Conventional)** blended learning system designed by UNY undergraduates:
* **Team:** Developed by an interdisciplinary team of three UNY students across Information Technology, Civic Education, and Automotive Engineering.
* **National Achievement:** Awarded **Juara 3 (3rd Place National)** in the Lomba Karya Tulis Ilmiah Nasional (Himapis LKTI 2021) organized by Himpunan Mahasiswa Pendidikan IPS FKIP Universitas Lampung.
* **Core Contribution:** Synchronous multi-stream classroom delivery uniting in-person and remote students simultaneously without timetable fragmentation during COVID-19 restricted schooling (*PTM Terbatas*).
