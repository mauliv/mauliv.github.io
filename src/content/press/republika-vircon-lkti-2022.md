## Overview

National news report published by Republika Online (*Republika Jogja*) covering the **Vircon Classroom** blended learning framework engineered by UNY students:
* **Authors:** Co-authored by three UNY students across Information Technology, Civic Education, and Automotive Engineering.
* **National Standing:** 3rd Place National Award (*Juara 3*) at the Lomba Karya Tulis Ilmiah Nasional Himapis FKIP Universitas Lampung 2021.
* **Key Innovation:** Addressed learning disparities caused by alternating shift models by integrating standard classroom audiovisual infrastructure with live web conferencing platforms (Zoom/Google Meet), ensuring continuous two-way teacher-student engagement across both physical and virtual cohorts.
