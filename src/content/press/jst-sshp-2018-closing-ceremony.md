## Official Report Overview

Official media coverage from the **Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST)** documenting the formal closing ceremony and farewell reception (*修了式＆歓送会*) for **Sakura Science High School Program (SSHP) 2018 Group 1**, held on **Friday, April 13, 2018** at JST Headquarters B1 Conference Hall in Chiyoda, Tokyo.

The delegation convened 108 high school students and supervisors across seven nations (Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Maldives, Sri Lanka, and Turkmenistan), accompanied by diplomatic representatives from respective national embassies, including the Embassy of the Republic of Indonesia (KBRI Tokyo).

### Ceremony Milestones Documented

1. **National Representative Speeches:** Student delegates took the stage for 3-minute reflective speeches on the week-long frontier science immersion across Japanese national laboratories and universities.
2. **Conferral of Certificates:** JST President **Hamaguchi Michinari, M.D., Ph.D.** personally conferred the official Certificates of Achievement (Certificate No. 18200073) and inducted all delegates into the worldwide **Sakura Science Club**.
3. **Cultural Performance:** Indonesian delegates performed a traditional Sundanese dance (*Manuk Dadali*) followed by *Indonesia Pusaka*, receiving warm standing ovations from international peers and diplomatic dignitaries.
