## Official Report Overview

Official media coverage from the **Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST)** documenting the academic visit of **Sakura Science High School Program (SSHP) 2018 Group 1** to **Shibaura Institute of Technology Kashiwa Junior & Senior High School** in Chiba Prefecture on **Monday, April 9, 2018**.

The cohort comprised 129 high school students and supervisors from seven Asian nations (India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Sri Lanka, Turkmenistan, Bangladesh, and Maldives).

### Keynote Lecture by Nobel Laureate Dr. Takaaki Kajita

* **Lecture Title:** *"Kamiokande: Starting point of my research"*
* **Keynote Speaker:** **Prof. Takaaki Kajita** (2015 Nobel Prize in Physics, Director of the Institute for Cosmic Ray Research, The University of Tokyo).
* **Scientific Insights:** Dr. Kajita detailed the foundational discoveries from the Super-Kamiokande underground observatory in Kamioka mine, Gifu Prefecture, establishing empirical proof that neutrinos possess non-zero mass via quantum flavor oscillations ($\nu_\mu \leftrightarrow \nu_\tau$).
* **Interactive Q&A:** Participating high school delegates engaged in rapid-fire technical Q&A regarding dark matter particles, subterranean Cherenkov detection, and neutrino mass transformations.
