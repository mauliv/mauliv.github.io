## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Governance

The **Pukyong National University International Summer School 2024 (PKNU-ISS 2024, Period 1)** was an intensive 9-day international mobility and academic program held from **July 24 to August 1, 2024** at Pukyong National University (국립부경대학교) in **Busan, South Korea**. Organized by PKNU’s **Office of International Affairs (OIA)** under Provost **Hyun Woo KIM, Ph.D.**, the program convened selected university delegations from around the world for a specialized dual curriculum blending formal classroom instruction, hands-on cultural workshops, and regional field expeditions across the metropolitan maritime hub of Busan.

Representing **Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (UNY)**, I was appointed as the **UNY Delegation Representative** (*Perwakilan Delegasi*) under formal university mandate (**Surat Keterangan Urgensi Kegiatan No. xxx/UN34.36/KI/2024**), issued by the UNY International Office. I attended on a **100% full partner scholarship** awarded by Pukyong National University (Letter of Acceptance dated May 31, 2024), which fully covered the program tuition fees, twin-room dormitory accommodation on the PKNU campus, daily meals, local transit, and all educational excursions.

Our contingent was part of a select national cohort of **17 student delegates across 7 prominent Indonesian higher education institutions** (UNY, Universitas Indonesia, IPB University, Universitas Jenderal Soedirman, Universitas Pendidikan Ganesha, Universitas Negeri Semarang, Universitas Mataram, and Universitas Negeri Malang). At UNY, the delegation consisted of a multidisciplinary two-student pairing from the Faculty of Engineering (Information Technology) and the Faculty of Economics & Business (Economics Education).

### Strategic Objectives Articulated in the KAK
1. **Academic & Intercultural Competence Development**:
   Enrolling in PKNU's accredited **Global Culture II** course (1 academic credit, equivalent to 15 hours of structured coursework), mastering foundational Korean language, traditional and contemporary physical arts, culinary anthropology, and Confucian social etiquette.
2. **Institutional Benchmarking for UNY Short Courses**:
   Serving as an operational scout to study, inspect, and benchmark how a leading South Korean national university structures, budgets, promotes, and executes high-profile international seasonal semesters. The empirical findings were commissioned to directly guide the design and scaling of UNY’s own incoming international mobility programs (such as the **EcoEduTech International Doctoral Summer School / ETSS**, **UNY International Summer School**, and **Darmasiswa / KNB Scholarships** cultural immersion modules).
3. **Bilateral Inter-University Diplomacy**:
   Strengthening direct organizational channels between the UNY International Office and PKNU's Office of International Affairs, advancing UNY’s World Class University (WCU) internationalization indicators, and exploring recurring faculty and student exchange pathways.
4. **Institutional Branding & Global Visibility**:
   Representing the university in formal international delegation showcases and inter-campus roundtables, projecting UNY’s academic heritage in teacher education, engineering, and digital campus administration.

## 3. Academic Curriculum & Core Modules: Global Culture II

The formal academic core centered on **Global Culture II** (Course Code: PKNU-ISS-GC2), a 1-credit intensive curriculum delivered entirely in English across morning (09:00–12:00) and afternoon (14:00–17:30) lecture and practicum blocks.

```
                  GLOBAL CULTURE II CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE
  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ 1. Korean Language & Communication                                    │
  │    Hangul phonetics, essential conversational etiquette & greetings   │
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 2. Traditional Martial Arts: Taekwondo (태권도)                        │
  │    Stances, philosophical discipline, respect ethics & self-defense   │
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 3. Contemporary Creative Arts: K-Pop Choreography                     │
  │    Global soft power, modern dance composition & teamwork dynamics    │
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 4. Culinary Anthropology: Korean Gastronomy                           │
  │    Nutritional philosophy, fermentation science & cuisine preparation │
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ 5. Confucian Social Ethics: Gijang Culture & Etiquette School         │
  │    Traditional Hanbok, Darye (tea ceremony) & bowing protocols        │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Korean Language & Everyday Phonetics**: Intensive survival Korean lessons focusing on Hangul articulation, respectful grammatical forms (*jondaenmal*), basic campus navigation phrases, and dining etiquette.
- **Taekwondo Masterclasses (태권도)**: Practical physical training led by certified martial arts masters at PKNU athletic facilities, covering traditional defensive blocks, balance control, formal bowing discipline (*kyorugi* ethics), and foundational kicking sequences.
- **K-Pop Dance & Performance Workshop**: Studio-based training in Building C23 exploring the cultural composition, synchronized rhythmic coordination, and global creative industry model underpinning contemporary South Korean cultural exports.
- **Korean Cuisine Practicum**: Hands-on culinary laboratory workshop examining the balance of *eumyangohaeng* (five elements and five colors in Korean food), preparing traditional staple dishes, and understanding the social traditions of shared dining.
- **Confucian Etiquette & Traditional Manners (Gijang School / 기장향교)**: Immersive workshop conducted at the historic Gijang Hyanggyo Confucian academy, where delegates wore traditional **Hanbok** (한복), practiced formal ancestral bowing techniques (*Jeol* / 절), and completed the meditative **Darye** (다례) tea ceremony emphasizing mindfulness and guest hospitality.

### Key Field Expeditions
- **Taejongdae Maritime Coastal Navigation (태종대)**:
  Boarded an offshore cruise vessel along the sheer cliff faces and marine wave-cut platforms of Taejongdae Park, studying Busan's natural breakwaters, lighthouse positioning, and the geological formations framing the Korea Strait.
- **National Maritime Museum of Korea (국립해양박물관)**:
  Curated academic exploration of the third-largest museum in South Korea, examining Korea's shipbuilding heritage, ancient navigation instruments, naval architectural models (including the historical *Geobukseon* turtle ship), and sustainable ocean conservation research.
- **Busan Cinema Infrastructure & Movie Museum (부산영화체험박물관)**:
  Guided inspection of Busan's creative economy infrastructure as the host of the Busan International Film Festival (BIFF) and Asia's first UNESCO Creative City of Film, analyzing digital media production exhibits, motion-capture stages, and film preservation technology.
- **Marine Leisure Sports Practicum**:
  Conducted along Busan's coastal waterfront, focusing on water safety standards, small-craft marine recreation, and public beach management under high-density summer visitor traffic.
- **Gamcheon Culture Village (감천문화마을)**:
  Case study in urban community regeneration, inspecting how a hillside settlement was transformed through community-led public murals, architectural preservation, and sustainable cultural tourism.

## 6. Institutional Benchmarking Insights for UNY Campus Operations

A pivotal deliverable mandated in the KAK was translating operational observations into concrete administrative takeaways for the **Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta International Office**, where I served as a Student Employee across 2021–2026:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               OPERATIONAL BENCHMARKING APPLIED AT UNY                  │
├─────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ PKNU-ISS Model          │ Translation to UNY International Operations  │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Dual-Track Modules   │ • Applied in ETSS 2024 doctoral workshop     │
│    (Theory + Fieldwork) │   (morning AI lectures + afternoon visits)   │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Dedicated OIA Buddy  │ • Standardized Liaison Officer (LO) SOP      │
│    Student Staff Fleet  │   for incoming international cohorts & VIPs  │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Single-Source Packet │ • Formulated consolidated arrival guidelines │
│    & Transit Logistics  │   for Yogyakarta E-Visa & housing clearance  │
├─────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Cultural Immersion   │ • Enhanced GCF & GLOSHIP festival itineraries│
│    with Living Heritage │   with authentic regional Javanese heritage  │
└─────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Dual-Track Scheduling Framework**:
   PKNU paired classroom modules in the morning with structured field expeditions in the afternoon. This balance maintained academic focus while giving foreign students contextualized exposure to the host country, a template directly mirrored in our coordination of the **EcoEduTech Doctoral Summer School (ETSS 2024)** at UNY.
2. **Student Liaison & "Buddy" Operational Fleet**:
   PKNU utilized trained domestic students to manage airport arrivals, campus navigation, language barriers, and room check-ins. Observing this workflow reinforced the necessity of our student liaison officer fleet at UNY, refining our briefing protocols for welcoming foreign researchers and scholarship cohorts.
3. **Streamlined Dormitory & Meal Management**:
   The provision of on-campus twin-room housing with private bathrooms, secure keycard access, and prepaid dining options minimized logistical friction, providing a model for UNY's international student housing agreements in Karangmalang.
4. **Cultural Immersion Through Authentic Institutions**:
   Partnering with specialized cultural bodies (such as the Gijang Confucian School and National Maritime Museum) ensured pedagogical depth rather than superficial tourism, demonstrating how UNY can better leverage regional institutions (such as the Yogyakarta Royal Palace / Kraton and local vocational workshops) in its own short-course offerings.
