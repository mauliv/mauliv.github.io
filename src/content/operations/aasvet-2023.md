## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Scope

The **19th Asian Academic Society for Vocational Education and Training Annual Conference (AASVET 2023)** was hosted at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta from **8 to 10 November 2023**, bringing together senior vocational education researchers, policy architects, and university rectors from South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Malaysia, and Indonesia.

The conference was presided over by AASVET President **Dr. Seung-Il Na** (Seoul National University) and hosted under the institutional auspices of Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta’s International Office and Faculty of Engineering. Under the theme *"Sustainable Innovative Strategies in Dealing with TVET Transformation for Digital TVET Improvement"*, the forum addressed Industry 4.0 integration, green TVET, digital pedagogical frameworks, and regional workforce policy.

***

## 2. Tiered Committee Structure & Candidate Mandate

Execution was managed through a formal **Tiered Committee Governance Framework**:

1. **Faculty Steering Committee**:
   Composed of AASVET executive leadership, university leadership, and senior engineering faculty. Responsible for academic paper selections, keynote panels, international bilateral agreements, and institutional governance.

2. **Student Operations Committee**:
   The frontline operational engine executing daily on-site logistics, delegate hospitality, multilingual registration desks, hybrid audio-visual broadcasting, and presentation room moderation.

3. **Candidate's Mandate — Student Committee Coordinator**:
   Under my formal Student Employment appointment at the UNY International Office, I served as the **Student Committee Coordinator**. In this capacity, I acted as the primary operational conduit between the senior faculty steering board and the student execution cohort, managing volunteer scheduling, live-streaming operations, and VIP delegate movements.

***

## 3. Operational Highlights & Field Execution

- **VIP Delegate Protocol & Escorts**: Directed student liaison teams welcoming foreign delegations from Chiba University (Japan), Seoul National University (South Korea), and regional partner institutions at Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) and coordinating official transit.
- **Hybrid Conference Infrastructure**: Supervised student technical operators managing live broadcast streams, slide desk operations, and remote participant Q&A across multi-track parallel academic sessions.
- **Academic Paper Presentation Rooms**: Coordinated room moderators and technical assistants managing scheduled paper presentations, timing protocols, and certificate distributions.
- **Cultural Gala & Hospitality Logistics**: Supervised floor arrangements and multilingual stage management for the conference welcoming dinner and cultural immersion excursions.
