## 1. Five-Plus Years of Higher Education & Campus Operations Experience

Five consecutive years (2021–present) serving as a Student Employee in the International Office at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (UNY). In Indonesian state universities, student employment is a structured campus staff role where students carry operational responsibility for departmental workflows alongside full-time university officers.

Rather than casual volunteering or short extracurricular projects, this role entailed continuous daily operational duties: managing statutory immigration compliance with the Indonesian government, issuing formal university letters of acceptance and financial guarantee, engaging and maintaining relationships with partner institutions abroad, hosting international delegations, and providing 24/7 emergency pastoral care for foreign students residing in Yogyakarta.

### Institutional Framework & Verifiable Annual Decrees

While operational outcomes, technical systems, and student welfare remain the focus of this portfolio, the university formalizes all student employment appointments through official annual decrees signed by university leadership (Rector's Executive Decrees), established under **University Presidential Regulation No. 3/2023 on the Student Employment Program**.

## 2. Regulatory State Systems & Immigration Processing

End-to-end processing of legal stay documentation for all incoming international students, researchers, and visiting faculty under Republic of Indonesia immigration laws:

- **E-Visa Applications**: Managing institutional sponsor accounts on the Directorate General of Immigration portal for Study Visas (C316), Visit Visas (B211A), and Academic Visas.
- **Limited Stay Permits (ITAS / KITAS)**: Coordinating in-person biometric registration and digital extension processing at the Yogyakarta Regional Immigration Office.
- **Police Notification Reports (STM)**: Submitting mandatory foreign resident notifications to the Provincial Police Intelligence Directorate within statutory deadlines.
- **Civil Registry Domicile Certificates (SKTT)**: Facilitating local civil registry residence filings with the regional Civil Registry Office for long-term degree students.
- **Departure & Status Changes (EPO / ERP)**: Processing Exit Permit Only (EPO) and Exit Re-entry Permits for graduating cohorts, study deferrals, and emergency repatriations.
- **Outbound Visa Facilitation**: Providing visa advisory and institutional recommendation letters for UNY academic delegations traveling abroad (Japan, Australia, South Korea, Schengen area).

## 3. Institutional Paperwork & Admissions Clearance

Managing the administrative pipeline that enables foreign nationals to legally enroll, reside, and study at the university:

- **Letters of Acceptance (LoA)**: Drafting official unconditional and conditional institutional LoAs for international degree applicants across bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs.
- **Sponsorship & Financial Guarantee Letters**: Issuing rector-authorized financial and behavioral guarantee documentation required by ministries and immigration authorities.
- **Scholarship Administration**: Maintaining administrative records for scholarship awardees under the Darmasiswa Program, the Developing Countries Partnership Scholarship (KNB), and internal UNY international tuition waivers.
- **SIMOBY Data Integration**: Serving as the lead operational user and data manager translating paper student files into UNY's internal mobility CRM (SIMOBY) and national clearinghouses, including the State Secretariat foreign clearance portal (SIMPEL).

Serving as the primary operational anchor and first-response contact for foreign student onboarding, welfare, health emergencies, and cultural transition in Yogyakarta:

### Welcoming Ceremony & Academic Orientation

Orchestrating arrival onboarding, official university welcoming ceremonies, and academic orientation for incoming foreign degree scholars (Darmasiswa Program, KNB Scholarship, UNY International Degree Scholarships) and foreign exchange students:

- **Orientation & Academic Briefing**: Organizing the formal university welcome ceremony at the UNY Rectorate Main Senate Hall, briefing incoming international cohorts on academic regulations, immigration statutory deadlines (e-ITAS stay permits, civil residency SKTT, police notification STM), and campus services.

- **Consular & Embassy Communication**: Assisting university leadership in drafting formal incident reports and diplomatic communications to foreign embassies and consulates in Jakarta.
- **Bereavement & Compassionate Care**: Managing formal administrative and consular protocols during acute family emergencies or sensitive welfare situations.

## 5. Overseas Partner Engagement, Delegation Hosting & Relationship Maintenance

International delegations do not happen automatically. Hosting overseas groups requires continuous communication with counterpart offices abroad, aligning academic expectations before arrival, and maintaining institutional relationships after visitors return home.

Operating under the university Directorate of Partnerships, Information Systems, Innovation, and Enterprise, operational responsibilities covered both initiating and sustaining inter-institutional cooperation:

### Sustaining Recurring Short-Course Partnerships

* **Nanyang Polytechnic (Singapore):** Served as operational contact across seven iterations of the campus immersion and cultural exchange program. Coordinated directly with NYP program managers on study tracks, community immersion schedules, student buddy allocations, and campus laboratory access.
* **Western Sydney University (Australia):** Managed pre-arrival arrangements, academic workshop scheduling, and local orientation across three cohorts of Australian students and accompanying faculty.
* **Post-Visit Follow-Through:** Collected program evaluations, shared debrief reports with overseas coordinators, and resolved administrative items to maintain multi-year collaboration channels.

### Bilateral Institutional Visits & Matchmaking

* **Bilateral Delegations:** Handled scheduling, campus tours, and bilateral dialogue logistics for official university visits from Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), Pukyong National University (PKNU), and Philippine Normal University (PNU).
* **High-Level Partnership Matchmaking (GLOSHIP 2025):** Coordinated the student liaison secretariat for UNY's Global Partnership Forum, managing protocol across 20 parallel bilateral roundtable rooms between international rectors, vice-chancellors, and ministry representatives where new MoUs, MoAs, and Implementation Agreements were finalized.
* **Inter-Faculty Coordination:** Connected visiting international delegations with relevant department chairs and deans across UNY to explore student exchange tracks, guest lectures, and joint research possibilities.
