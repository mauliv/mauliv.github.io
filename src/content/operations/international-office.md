## 1. Five-Plus Years of Higher Education & Campus Operations Experience

Five consecutive years (2021–present) serving as a Student Employee in the International Office at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta. In Indonesian state universities (PTN-BH), student employment (*Program Mahasiswa Kerja*) is a structured campus staff role where students carry operational responsibility for departmental workflows alongside full-time university officers.

Rather than casual volunteering or short extracurricular projects, this role entailed continuous daily operational duties: managing statutory immigration compliance with the Indonesian government, issuing formal university letters of acceptance and financial guarantee, hosting international VIP delegations, and providing 24/7 emergency pastoral care for foreign students residing in Yogyakarta.

### Institutional Framework & Verifiable Annual Decrees

While operational outcomes, technical systems, and student welfare remain the focus of this portfolio, the university formalizes all student employment appointments through official annual decrees signed by university leadership (*Surat Keputusan Rektor UNY*), established under **Peraturan Rektor No. 3 Tahun 2023 tentang Program Mahasiswa Kerja**.

## 2. Regulatory State Systems & Immigration Processing

End-to-end processing of legal stay documentation for all incoming international students, researchers, and visiting faculty under Republic of Indonesia immigration laws:

- **E-Visa Applications**: Managing institutional sponsor accounts on the Directorate General of Immigration portal for Study Visas (C316), Visit Visas (B211A), and Academic Visas.
- **Limited Stay Permits (ITAS / KITAS)**: Coordinating in-person biometric registration and digital extension processing at Kantor Imigrasi Kelas I TPI Yogyakarta.
- **Police Notification Reports (STM / Surat Tanda Melapor)**: Submitting mandatory foreign resident notifications to the Directorate of Intelligence and Security (Ditintelkam Polda DIY) within statutory deadlines.
- **Civil Registry Domicile (SKTT / Surat Keterangan Tempat Tinggal)**: Facilitating local civil registry filings with Dinas Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil (Dukcapil) for long-term degree students.
- **Departure & Status Changes (EPO / ERP)**: Processing Exit Permit Only (EPO) and Exit Re-entry Permits for graduating cohorts, study deferrals, and emergency repatriations.
- **Outbound Visa Facilitation**: Providing visa advisory and institutional recommendation letters for UNY academic delegations traveling abroad (Japan, Australia, South Korea, Schengen area).

## 3. Institutional Paperwork & Admissions Clearance

Managing the administrative pipeline that enables foreign nationals to legally enroll, reside, and study at the university:

- **Letters of Acceptance (LoA)**: Drafting official unconditional and conditional institutional LoAs for international degree applicants across bachelor's, master's, and doctoral programs.
- **Sponsorship & Financial Guarantee Letters**: Issuing rector-authorized financial and behavioral guarantee documentation required by ministries and immigration authorities.
- **Scholarship Administration**: Maintaining administrative records for scholarship awardees under Darmasiswa RI, Kemitraan Negara Berkembang (KNB), and internal UNY international tuition waivers.
- **SIMOBY Data Integration**: Serving as the lead operational user and data manager translating paper student files into UNY's internal mobility CRM (SIMOBY) and national clearinghouses (SIMPEL Kemensetneg).

## 4. International Student Welcoming, Academic Orientation & Pastoral Care

Serving as the primary operational anchor and first-response contact for foreign student onboarding, welfare, health emergencies, and cultural transition in Yogyakarta:

### Welcoming Ceremony & Academic Orientation (Mahasiswa Internasional)

Orchestrating arrival onboarding, official university welcoming ceremonies, and academic orientation for incoming foreign degree scholars (Darmasiswa RI, KNB Scholarship, UNY International Degree Scholarships) and foreign exchange students:

- **Orientation & Academic Briefing**: Organizing the formal university welcome ceremony at Ruang Sidang Utama Rektorat, briefing incoming international cohorts on academic regulations, immigration statutory deadlines (ITAS/SKTT/STM), and campus services.
- **Student Liaison Officer (LO) Coordination**: Directing student liaison officers in airport arrivals, campus navigation, and immediate logistical onboarding.
- **Cultural Transition & Housing Assistance**: Guiding incoming students through local residential contracts (*kos-kosan* / dormitories), Indonesian SIM card registrations, local bank account openings, and cross-cultural acclimatization.

### Student Welfare, Crisis & Emergency Response

- **24/7 Emergency Support**: On-call liaison for medical emergencies, sudden hospitalizations, and accidents involving foreign students, coordinating with university clinics and regional hospitals.
- **Consular & Embassy Communication**: Assisting university leadership in drafting formal incident reports and diplomatic communications to foreign embassies and consulates in Jakarta.
- **Bereavement & Compassionate Care**: Managing formal administrative and consular protocols during acute family emergencies or sensitive welfare situations.
