## 1. Institutional Context & Mandate

The **Global Partnership Forum (GLOSHIP UNY 2025)** was convened on **22–23 April 2025** at Auditorium UNY by the Vice Rectorate for Research, Cooperation, Information Systems, and Business (Bidang RKSIU) alongside the International Office.

Organised under the theme:

> *“Moving Multi-Helix Synergies Forward for the Stronger Global Partnership”*

The forum was designed to establish tangible collaborative networks across universities, global industries, non-governmental organisations, and government agencies. The plenary assembly featured an official keynote address by the **Minister of Higher Education, Science, and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia (Mendiktisaintek RI) Prof. Brian Yuliarto**, followed by institutional leaders from the University of Exeter (United Kingdom), University of Melbourne (Australia), Universiti Malaya (Malaysia), University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP), IPB University, and Chinese-Indonesian cultural exchange bodies.

***

Under my student employment mandate at the International Office, I served as the **Koordinator Panitia Mahasiswa (Student Operational Committee Coordinator)**. My core responsibility was establishing and directing the **Student Liaison Officer (LO) fleet** that powered the 20 simultaneous matchmaking roundtables and VIP delegate experiences.

***

## 3. The 20-Room Matchmaking Roundtable Architecture

The core operational innovation of GLOSHIP 2025 was the **Matchmaking Roundtable Sessions** — 20 parallel rooms pairing Indonesian faculties with foreign universities, industry consortia, and diplomatic missions:

| Operational Component | Scope & Specifications |
|:---|:---|
| **Roundtable Tracks** | 20 simultaneous bilateral & multilateral sessions across Auditorium UNY and Graduate School |
| **Student LO Assignment** | Dedicated **Mahasiswa LO** assigned per room to handle attendance, document distribution, and liaison |
| **Protocol Briefing** | Authored standard operating procedures (SOP) for student LOs: diplomatic greeting, timing discipline, minute-taking |
| **Legal Documentation** | Coordinated signing ceremony workflows for **MoU (Memorandum of Understanding)**, **MoA (Memorandum of Agreement)**, and **IA (Implementation Arrangement)** documents |

Each roundtable had an assigned Faculty Host, Faculty Notetaker, and a designated **Mahasiswa LO (Student Liaison Officer)** operating under my direct operational oversight. This ensured that diplomatic discussions translated immediately into structured minutes and bilateral agreements without procedural delay.

***

## 4. Operational Leadership & Student Coordination Scope

### A. Diplomatic Protocol & VIP Guest Escort
- Briefed and deployed student liaison officers to receive international rectors, vice-chancellors, and ministerial aides upon campus arrival.
- Managed motorcade parking sequences, VIP green-room staging, and escort pathways between Auditorium UNY and Gedung Pascasarjana.

### B. Parallel Session Synchronisation
- Maintained a unified command network coordinating 20 concurrent rooms, enforcing strict 45-minute discussion cycles and transitional breaks.
- Deployed floating runners to resolve technical, audiovisual, or protocol adjustments in real time across the two venues.

### C. MoU / MoA Signing Secretariat
- Pre-staged bilateral signing folios, institutional seals, and executive pens for ceremonial signing rounds on the main auditorium stage following the ministerial keynote.

### D. Multi-Helix Innovation Expo & Cultural City Tour
- Supervised student crew members managing exhibition booths for university research centres, startups, and community partners.
- Organised operational logistics for the cultural city tour showcasing Yogyakarta's heritage to visiting international delegates.

***

## 5. Institutional Significance

GLOSHIP UNY 2025 transitioned UNY from standard bilateral visits to a true **multi-lateral partnership clearinghouse**:

- **Cabinet-Level Visibility**: Successfully staged an international forum featuring the sitting Mendiktisaintek RI.
- **Measurable Bilateral Pipeline**: Generated actionable Implementation Agreements (IA) across faculties and international partners.
- **Repeatable Operational Model**: Proved that student employment at the International Office can coordinate complex diplomatic protocol through disciplined tiered committee structures.
