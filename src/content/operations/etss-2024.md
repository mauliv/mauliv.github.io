## 1. Executive Summary & Programme Scope

The **EcoEduTech International Doctoral Summer School 2024 (ETSS 2024)** was an intensive 5-day academic programme held from **7 to 11 October 2024** at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta. The summer school convened doctoral candidates, postdoctoral researchers, and distinguished professors from Europe and Asia to explore the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in sustainable education.

Supported by a global university consortium—including **TU Dresden (Germany)**, **University of Bergen (Norway)**, **University of Strasbourg (France)**, **Pokhara University (Nepal)**, and **Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (Indonesia)**—the programme featured expert lectures, interactive doctoral colloquiums, ethical AI policy debates, and cross-border collaborative research sessions.

***

## 2. Tiered Committee Structure & Candidate Mandate

Execution followed the university’s **Tiered Committee Governance Framework**:

1. **Faculty Steering Committee**:
   Chaired by **Prof. Dr. Mochamad Bruri Triyono** (Head of the UNY International Office) alongside visiting European and Asian academic directors. Responsible for curriculum design, doctoral paper reviews, keynote lectures, and inter-institutional treaties.

2. **Student Operations Committee**:
   The frontline operational engine managing daily lecture venues, digital presentation equipment, delegate transport, catering logistics, and doctoral research showcase spaces.

3. **Candidate's Mandate — Student Committee Coordinator**:
   Under my formal Student Employment mandate at the UNY International Office, I served as the **Student Committee Coordinator**. In this capacity, I directed the student operational team across all five days of intensive programming, coordinating closely with international faculty and ensuring seamless operational support for visiting foreign scholars.

***

## 3. Operational Highlights & Field Execution

- **Doctoral Workshop & Seminar Coordination**: Managed room configurations, digital projectors, and breakout roundtable spaces at the UNY Rectorate Main Senate Hall and Graduate School for parallel thematic tracks.
- **International Scholar Liaison & Hospitality**: Oversaw student liaison officers providing 24/7 logistical support for visiting professors and doctoral fellows from Germany, Norway, France, and Nepal, including hotel-to-campus transit and local orientations.
- **AI Demo & Technical Session Support**: Ensured reliable high-bandwidth campus network connections and AV hardware for interactive AI-assisted educational tool demonstrations.
- **Cultural Immersion & Field Excursions**: Planned and escorted visiting European and Asian scholars on educational and cultural excursions around the Special Region of Yogyakarta, managing schedules and safety protocols.
