## 1. Institutional Context & Mandate

The **Global Culture Festival (GCF)** is Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta’s annual flagship internationalisation event, organised by the International Office to foster intercultural harmony, celebrate global diversity, and showcase the rich heritage of UNY's international student community.

The 11th edition of GCF was convened on **3 December 2024** at the Gelanggang Olah Raga (GOR) UNY under the banner:

> *“Nurturing Global Harmony: Celebrating International Friendship”*

The festival was officially opened by the Rector of UNY, **Prof. Sumaryanto**, alongside Vice Rector for Research, Cooperation, Information Systems, Innovation, and Business **Prof. Margana**, and Head of the International Office **Prof. Mochamad Bruri Triyono**. The gathering welcomed over **100 international students from 35 nations**, campus leadership, student organisations (ormawa/UKM), and regional dignitaries including the Yogyakarta Tourism Office.

***

In this role, I served as the critical operational bridge between faculty leadership and the student execution corps, translating strategic directives from the Head of the International Office into concrete ground operations.

## 3. Operational Coordination Scope

Managing an arena-scale festival with participants from 35 countries required multi-vector coordination across five operational divisions:

### A. International Participant Logistics & Country Booths
- Coordinated with international degree, Darmasiswa, and developing countries partnership (KNB) scholarship students to establish **country-specific cultural pavilions**.
- Facilitated exhibition requirements, electricity distribution, booth layouts, and culinary safety protocols for international food showcases.

### B. Traditional Costume Parade & Stage Protocol
- Directed the grand opening **cultural parade**, synchronising the sequence of 35 national delegations wearing traditional attire.
- Managed stage queues, multilingual master of ceremony cues, and protocol seating for university executives and government guests.

### C. Student Volunteer Fleet Management
- Mobilised and scheduled a corps of **40+ student volunteers and liaison officers (LOs)** drawn from UNY student activity units.
- Assigned dedicated LOs to international contingents with non-English or limited Indonesian proficiency, ensuring smooth communication and pastoral care throughout the event.

### D. Crowd Control & Arena Safety
- Coordinated safety perimeters, emergency egress routes, and registration flows at GOR UNY accommodating **1,500+ visitors** (students, faculty, local schools, and public visitors).
