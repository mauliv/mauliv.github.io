## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Collaboration

The **5th International Conference on Teacher Education and Professional Development (InCoTEPD 2024)** was convened on **25–26 June 2024** at the **Auditorium of Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta**. The international gathering was organized by UNY in formal collaboration with the **Southeast Asian Ministers of Education Organization (SEAMEO)**—represented by SEAMEO QITEP in Science and SEAMEO QITEP in Language—and the **Jogja English Teachers Association (JETA)**.

Centred around the theme ***"Teachers' Professional Development in the Age of AI"***, the conference served as a premier regional platform for educational researchers, teacher educators, policymakers, and classroom practitioners exploring the pedagogical impact, ethical boundaries, and digital competencies demanded by rapid Artificial Intelligence adoption in 21st-century classrooms.

### Distinguished Keynote Speakers

The plenary sessions featured leading international and national authorities in higher education and educational technology:
- **Prof. Dr. Ir. Sri Suning Kusumawardani, S.T., M.T.** — Director of Learning and Student Affairs, Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia (Kemendikbudristek RI)
- **Prof. Chris Ziguras** — University of Melbourne, Australia
- **Prof. Soni Nopembri, Ph.D.** — Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta, Indonesia
- **Dr. A. Gumawang Jati** — President of Indonesia Technology Enhanced Language Learning (i-TELL)
- **Reza Setiawan, M.T.** — SEAMEO Regional Centre for QITEP in Science
- **Associate Professor Hairon Salleh** — National Institute of Education (NIE), Singapore
- **Dr. Pelin Derinalp** — Gaziantep University, Turkey
- **Dr. Yasemin Yelbay Yilmaz** — Hacettepe University, Turkey
- **Dr. Wahyudi** — Director of SEAMEO Secretariat
- **Dr. Kokoy Siti Komariah, M.T., M.Eng.** — National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN)

## 2. Tiered Committee Structure & Candidate Mandate

In line with UNY's standard international event management model, execution was organized through a two-tier governance framework:

1. **Faculty Steering Committee**:
   Composed of UNY university executives, faculty deans, and SEAMEO regional directors. This committee oversaw ministerial protocol, academic peer reviews, conference keynote agreements, and international organizational treaties.

2. **Student Operations Committee**:
   The primary execution body handling on-the-ground event delivery, including plenary auditorium floor management, technical audiovisual support, multilingual registration desks, delegate hospitality, and parallel session timing.

3. **Candidate's Mandate — Student Committee Coordinator**:
   Appointed under my formal Student Employment mandate at the UNY International Office, I served as the **Student Committee Coordinator**. In this role, I served as the operational interface between the faculty steering committee and the student crew, orchestrating workforce deployment across plenary sessions, technical desks, and parallel academic presentation rooms.

***

## 3. Operational Highlights & Field Execution

- **Plenary Auditorium Operations**: Supervised venue setup, stage flow, VIP seating arrangements, and timing controls for keynote presentations inside the 1,500+ seat UNY Auditorium.
- **Hybrid Conference & Audiovisual Desks**: Directed student technical operators running multi-camera livestreams, remote keynote feeds, and dynamic presentation switching for international presenters joining from Australia, Singapore, and Turkey.
- **International Speaker & VIP Liaison**: Managed dedicated student liaison officers tasked with airport transfers, hotel escorts, protocol assistance, and campus itineraries for visiting foreign scholars and ministry delegates.
- **Academic Parallel Presentation Desks**: Organized breakout rooms across campus buildings for peer-reviewed paper presentations, briefing student session moderators on timing cue cards, attendance verification, and digital certificate distribution.
- **Registration & Delegate Secretariat**: Oversaw digital attendee badge scanning, kit distribution, and on-site inquiry desks handling hundreds of educators, researchers, and university delegates over the two-day summit.
