## Undergraduate Thesis

Empirical evaluation of the SIMOBY international mobility CRM (`simoby.uny.ac.id`) using WebQual 4.0 and Importance-Performance Analysis (IPA). Audited **830 outbound PDLN submissions** (**641** from **303** faculty/staff and **189** from **153** students) across **456 unique proposers** (2022–2026), surveyed **87 end users** (Performance) and **12 internal stakeholders** (Importance) across **22 WebQual 4.0 indicators** (Cronbach's Alpha `0.964` / `0.948`; Mean Performance `3.358` vs. Importance `3.595`), isolated **3 Quadrant I priority gaps** (`US2`, `SI6`, `SI7`), and authored **5 conceptual UI/UX redesign mockups** alongside an engineering action plan.
