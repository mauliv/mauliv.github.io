## Program Overview

IBM SkillsBuild University Education is a collaborative program between **IBM** and **Hacktiv8 Indonesia** aimed at equipping young talents with practical capabilities in Artificial Intelligence. The "IT - AI Agent for Programming" track focused on designing, building, and deploying AI agents to solve real-world problems and automate software engineering workflows.

## Competencies & Modules

1. **Foundation & AI Introduction (3 Hours / 4 JP):**
   - Fundamentals of Large Language Models (LLMs) and their architectural applications.
   - Using AI developer tools to troubleshoot, debug, and improve code.
   - Applying model assistance to solve algorithmic challenges and technical problems.

2. **AI Agent Development & Workflow Integration (3 Hours / 4 JP):**
   - Fundamentals of AI agent design, prompt chains, memory, and tool calling.
   - Automation and orchestration concepts for agentic systems.
   - Hands-on practice using AI agent tools and frameworks (IBM watsonx.ai).

3. **Project Building & Capstone Prototype (3 Hours / 4 JP):**
   - Developed an end-to-end AI Agent capstone prototype using Langflow and agent orchestration pipelines.
   - Applied workflow automation to real-world software engineering challenges.

## Evaluation & Project Score

The final capstone project was independently assessed across 4 key dimensions, achieving a **Final Score of 90 / 100**:

- **Completeness & Feasibility (Weight: 40%):** Score **91**  
  *Participant's ability to develop a complete end-to-end solution, demonstrate logical flow between components, create relevant prompts and configurations, produce outputs aligned with objectives, and show that the solution is feasible to execute.*
- **Creativity & Innovation (Weight: 25%):** Score **88**  
  *Participant's ability to demonstrate originality, explore innovative ideas, develop unique flows or integrations, and present solutions that provide additional value.*
- **System Design & Architecture (Weight: 15%):** Score **89**  
  *Participant's ability to clearly design and explain system architecture, define structured workflows from input to output, demonstrate logical integration between components, and create scalable and modular solution designs.*
- **Background & Impact (Weight: 20%):** Score **92**  
  *Participant's ability to demonstrate an understanding of real-world problems, clearly explain project objectives, link the solution to user benefits, and show clear cause-and-effect thinking.*
