## Micro-Credential

**Indonesian Student Micro-Credential (KMMI):** Scrum Project Management certification course delivered by the Ministry of Education (Kemdikbudristek RI) and UNY (Aug – Oct 2021).

## Curriculum

Agile workflows, Sprint planning, Scrum roles, backlog management, and retrospectives: the methodology layer applied across subsequent project leadership (RESTek, SIMOBY liaison work).
