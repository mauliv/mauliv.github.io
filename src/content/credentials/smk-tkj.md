## 1. Vocational Engineering Foundation (TKJ)

Graduated with an academic average score of **85.3 / 100** from **SMK Negeri 3 Yogyakarta** (Skagata / ex-STM 2 Jetis), specializing in **Teknik Komputer dan Jaringan (TKJ)**. 

The curriculum provided rigorous hands-on Layer 1 through Layer 4 infrastructure training:
* **Physical Infrastructure:** Structured UTP and optical fiber termination, OTDR link testing, and rack-mount server assembly.
* **Network Topologies & Enterprise Routing:** Subnet planning (VLSM), VLAN segmentation, inter-VLAN routing, and static/dynamic routing on Cisco and MikroTik hardware.
* **Server Administration:** Linux (Debian/Ubuntu) deployment, DNS/DHCP configuration, Apache/Nginx web serving, and SSH remote management.

---

## 2. Capstone Project: XigmaNAS Network Storage

For the final technical assessment, designed and deployed an enterprise **Network Attached Storage (NAS)** system powered by **XigmaNAS** (FreeBSD-based ZFS architecture):
* Configured software RAID storage pools to ensure data parity, integrity checks, and fault tolerance.
* Implemented Samba/SMB and NFS network shares with granular role-based access control (RBAC) across departmental user groups.
* Benchmarked throughput across Gigabit Ethernet uplinks to validate multi-user concurrent read/write performance.

## 3. Early Software Engineering: School-Wide E-Voting (`pemilos.skagata.sch.id`)

In August 2018, pioneered digital electoral governance for the school by architecting and launching the **Online E-Voting System (Pemilos)** for Student Council (**OSIS**) and Vice-Principal elections:
* **Production Domain:** Deployed on the official school subdomain at `www.pemilos.skagata.sch.id`.
* **Tech Stack:** Native PHP and MySQL with an intuitive responsive web interface.
* **Impact & Institutional Longevity:** Replaced thousands of physical paper ballots with real-time digital voting across networked computer laboratories. The platform continued to be trusted and actively utilized by the school for years after graduation, as formally documented in the school's official coverage:

> *"Proses pemungutan suara Pemilihan Ketua OSIS dilakukan secara daring melalui aplikasi yang dapat diakses pada laman www.pemilos.skagata.sch.id . Aplikasi ini merupakan karya dari Alifah Maulidin Nur Ikhsan, alumni TKJ lulusan tahun 2019. Aplikasi ini pertama ksli diciptakan semasa belajar di SMK Negeri 3 Yogyakarta."*  
> — **[SMK Negeri 3 Yogyakarta Official Portal (April 2021)](https://smkn3jogja.sch.id/2021/04/pelantikan-pengurus-osis-smk-n-3-yogyakarta-masa-bakti-tahun-2021/)**

This early high-concurrency production deployment established the foundational database architecture, atomic vote commitment, and tamper-evident auditing workflows that would later evolve into UNY's enterprise-scale campus e-voting engine (**[pemilwa.uny.ac.id](/it/pemilwa-evoting)**).
