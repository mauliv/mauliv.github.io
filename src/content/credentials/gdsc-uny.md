## 1. Executive Scope

Chapter Lead and Event Director of Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC): UNY Chapter, the official Google Developers student community at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (Aug 2020 – Aug 2022).

## 2. Community Programming

Spearheaded the chapter's signature "Code, Collab, Commit" initiative, organizing workshops, hands-on coding sessions, and speaker panels covering Web Development, Android, and Cloud Computing to connect academic foundations directly to practical software delivery.

## 3. Leadership Model

Two-year elected tenure running the chapter's event pipeline, speaker relations, and study-group curriculum.
